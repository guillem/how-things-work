# Review: gradient-descent

## Verdict
The page is ready after the fixes below. The algorithm and its constants are right: plain descent, heavy-ball momentum, Gaussian noise and mini-batch SGD are all implemented correctly. I reran the bowl, ravine, plateau and valleys landscapes on their own and they reproduce the numbers the text quotes: about 100, 190 and 30 steps in the ravine, a loss of 0.43 in the second valley, the plateau's well curvature of 1.1 and limit of 1.8, and the noise step's "sometimes deepest, sometimes the other". I could not rerun the line fit, because `makeData` from the learning-from-data page was not supplied, so its numbers rest on the page's own tests. The most important fix is in the playground: the noise slider does nothing on the line-fit landscape, yet the readouts say noise is on and call the ball "wandering". After that, the momentum step explains momentum with a cancelling zigzag that the reader cannot see at the η it uses.

## Factual errors
- **low**, steps.ts:212: "Adam combine[s] momentum with a separate step size for each parameter, which also tames ravines." The first half is correct: Adam "computes individual adaptive learning rates for different parameters from estimates of first" and second moments. The second half is only true for ravines that run along a parameter axis. A diagonal preconditioner rescales coordinate axes; it cannot rotate them. The page's ravine is turned by 30°. Fix: "…with a separate step size for each parameter, which tames ravines that run along a parameter's axis." Sources: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.6980v1, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.22145
- The other facts check out. Polyak's 1964 heavy ball is correct (https://www.researchgate.net/publication/243648538). So are the stability limit η < 2/λ, the claim about saddle-point plateaus (Dauphin et al. 2014), and the explanations of convexity, epochs and learning-rate schedules.

## Model issues
- **medium**, descent.ts:334 together with steps.ts:277 and DescentScene.svelte:43/250–261: `sigma` is forced to 0 when `land.id === 'line'`. The playground offers the noise slider on every landscape, though, and does not offer `batch`. So when a reader sets noise on the line fit, nothing happens. The settings readout still shows "noise 1", `noisy` is true, the trail is cut to its last 40 steps, and `verdict` says "wandering around the bottom" until the run settles. Fix: apply the added noise whenever the full batch is used:
  `const sigma = batch < examples.length ? 0 : (o.noise ?? 0);`
  Then update the header comment at descent.ts:9–12 to match. Alternatively, in `setup` set `noise = 0` when `landId === 'line'` and grey out the control.
- **low**, e2e test lines 148–179: the tests only loosely bound the step counts the narrative quotes. They check "> 150" where the text says ~190, and "< plain/4" where it says ~30. Nothing tests the 0.43 loss in the valleys step or the claim that β = 0.95 "circles… before settling" (it settles at step 150 at η = 0.9). Add `expect(wells.minima[1].value).toBeCloseTo(0.43, 2)`, a check that `settledAt` falls within 175–205 and 25–35, and a check that `descend(ravine, ravine.start, {lr: 0.9, momentum: 0.95}).settledAt > 0`.
- Otherwise the model holds up: Box–Muller, the partial Fisher–Yates mini-batch, the Newton refinement of the minima and the handling of divergence are sound, and the gradients are tested against finite differences.

## Narrative and scene mismatches
- **medium**, steps.ts:210: "Across the ravine the pushes alternate left and right and largely cancel." At this step's η = 0.9, plain descent does not zigzag at all. The across-floor offset goes 1.47 → 0.15 → 0.015, because the factor is 1 − 0.9 = +0.1. With β = 0.8 it is the momentum itself that makes the ball swing across: 1.47, 0.15, −1.05, −1.06, −0.12, 0.74 … So the reader sees the opposite of the sentence. Fix: "Along the floor every push points the same way, so with momentum they add up to real speed. Across the ravine, as soon as the ball swings past the floor, the push reverses and brakes it, so the swinging dies away instead of building up." Another option is to keep η = 1.8 from the previous step: I measured 98 steps plain and 30 with β = 0.8, but then "about 190" must change to "about 100".
- **medium**, steps.ts:225: "Slide β to 0.8 and it gets off the plateau in about a quarter of the steps." This matches the loss plot: the loss drops below 0.45 at step 123 plain and step 33 with β = 0.8. But the only number on screen is the verdict "reached the bottom of the well at step N", which reads 125 against 59, about half. Fix: "…it drops into the well after about 30 steps instead of about 120, though its speed then carries it round the bottom for a while before it settles."
- **low**, steps.ts:174, DescentScene.svelte:267/271, descent.ts:19: "moves the ball to (1 − η) times its distance from the bottom" and the readout "distance to the bottom × (1 − η) = × −0.9". A distance cannot become negative. Fix steps.ts:174: "a step multiplies the ball's offset from the bottom by (1 − η): its distance shrinks by the size of that factor, and a negative factor puts it on the other side." Fix the readout: `each step: offset from the bottom × (1 − η) = × {fmt(1 - s.lr)}`. Fix descent.ts:19: "by exactly |1 − η|".
- **low**, steps.ts:175–180: the slider offers η = 2 (LR_VALUES), but none of the bullets covers it. The ball then bounces forever between (−2.1, 1.5) and (2.1, −1.5), and the readout says "still going after 300 steps". Add: "<li>η = 2: the factor is −1, and the ball bounces between two points forever;</li>"
- **low**, steps.ts:195: the ball zigzags "because the gradient points mostly across the ravine". The zigzag actually comes from overshooting across the floor (η > 1/1, so the across factor is 1 − 1.8 = −0.8). The direction of the gradient explains the slow progress, not the zigzag. Fix: "At η = 1.8 every step overshoots the narrow floor, so the ball bounces from wall to wall in a zigzag; and because the gradient points mostly across the ravine, not along it, each step gains only a sliver along the floor…"
- **low**, steps.ts:162: "needs dozens of steps to get anywhere" overstates it. The ball is halfway down by about step 14 and settles at step 74. Fix: "the ball crawls, and needs about 70 steps to reach the bottom."
- The other quoted numbers match the model: about 100 steps at η = 1.8, about 190 and about 30 steps with momentum, a loss of 0.43, a curvature of 1.1 with limit 1.8, divergence on the line fit past η = 1, 0.118, and noise ending "sometimes" in the deepest valley. At σ = 1, 23 of 40 seeds ended in the deepest valley and 16 in the shallower one. The playground challenge of under 30 steps is achievable: η = 2.1, β = 0.7 settles at step 18.

## Catalogue requirements
Everything is met. The interaction is built as written: a surface and a contour map, steps along −∇L, learning rate, momentum, noise, and the ravine, plateau and several-minima landscapes. The takeaway (crawls, converges, diverges) shows on its own in the compare step's three balls and loss curves, and the links to the calculus and learning-from-data prerequisites are present.

## Clarity
- **low**, steps.ts:236: "the ball goes down into the valley it happens to be above" is ambiguous on a map where "above" means "higher up the picture". The ball starts at the top and moves down and to the left. Fix: "the ball rolls into the nearest valley downhill from it, not the deepest one".
